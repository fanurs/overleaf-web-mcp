Connect your existing Overleaf login

This uses zero cloud-browser minutes. No timer starts.

  1. Sign into Overleaf in this browser.
  2. Open and sign into your private setup page using the same setup account as before.
  3. Check the project URL below and click Connect.

https://overleaf-mcp.fanurs.com/connect

Connect sends only Overleaf cookies directly to the private service above, which stores them encrypted. Cookies grant account access, not just access to this project. No password or Google/university cookies are read. Nothing is sent to chat or saved in this extension.

Ready when you are. Keep this popup open until it finishes.

You can remove this extension after connecting. Repeat the transfer only if the saved session expires. This is a connection test; the editing MCP is not ready yet.
